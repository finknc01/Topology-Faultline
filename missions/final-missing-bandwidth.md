# Final Case — The Missing Bandwidth

## Briefing
You receive two redacted node dossiers. The BOMs match. One node underperforms in distributed training and storage staging.

## Challenge
Create two synthetic but plausible topology dossiers yourself (or have another person/tool generate them without showing you the answer). Keep CPU/GPU/NIC/NVMe model names identical, but introduce one or two topology differences.

## Required work
1. Reconstruct both topologies from the evidence packet.
2. Identify the meaningful differences.
3. Predict which workload classes would notice them.
4. Propose commands/measurements that would prove the hypothesis on real hardware.
5. Write a short forensic report with confidence level and remaining unknowns.

## Final evidence
- both reconstructed diagrams
- evidence table
- root-cause argument
- production validation plan
- section separating measured facts from model assumptions

## Victory condition
You can explain why identical inventory does not imply identical topology or performance—and prove your reasoning without hand-waving.
