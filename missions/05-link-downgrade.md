# Case 05 — The Link That Lied

## Briefing
Inventory reports the correct hardware. Performance telemetry says something is wrong. The link is up, so monitoring calls it healthy.

## Objective
Practice diagnosing degraded rather than failed topology.

## Scenario
Analyze a clearly labeled synthetic incident in which one device negotiates fewer lanes or a lower generation than designed. Add at least two competing hypotheses: platform bifurcation/slot design, firmware/BIOS configuration, signal integrity/hardware issue, power state, or device limitation.

## Tasks
Create a “designed vs observed” table. Identify the exact evidence that would confirm or eliminate each hypothesis. Estimate the ceiling imposed by the degraded link.

## Evidence
- hypothesis matrix
- bandwidth estimate
- escalation checklist for a data-center technician

## Victory condition
You can turn a vague complaint of “the GPU is slow” into a topology-specific investigation plan.
